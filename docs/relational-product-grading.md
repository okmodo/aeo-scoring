# Relational product grading

The fact grader recognizes three additional source-bound relationships: a named product explicitly styled for a specific activity audience can establish its purpose; product-associated articulated or body-mapped panels can establish a specific feature; and those panels explicitly accommodating the wearer's shape can establish specific PFD fit guidance. These checks operate on already validated, source-linked facts and do not add model roles or source evidence.

The checks require an explicit product and relationship in the same clause. They do not promote generic panel headings, unrelated products, negated statements, or relationships assembled across disconnected clauses. The score retains the actual contributing source evidence. Missing supporting attributes remain unmeasured even if one feature is established.

The R14 model response and runtime result are historical evidence and remain unchanged. Any replay under this revision is an offline grading result; it is not a new live invocation or a measurement of this revision's runtime.
