# Independent fallback coverage review

The deterministic fallback previously assigned policy zero from a complete product region and absence of a known link even when policy coverage was unknown. It also assigned zero for an empty read policy without requiring complete product coverage. Both paths could mistake incomplete inspection for confirmed missing information, contrary to the canonical C3 scoring and coverage contract.

The revised negative path requires a safe, complete product region and a safe, complete, read policy with no nonempty readable policy blocks; product text with recognizable policy cues also prevents a zero. Missing, failed or incomplete policy reads, truncated or unsafe sources, incomplete product content, policy cues and nonempty unrecognized policy content remain unknown. Explicit positive policy-term scoring is unchanged. The review caught an intermediate guard that could treat a nonempty implicit policy such as “All purchases are final” as absence; the final guard and regression preserve unknown for such wording. As with other deterministic text rules, cue recognition is not a general semantic guarantee for arbitrary product prose.
