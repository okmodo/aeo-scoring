# Independent acceptance matrix

Frozen from the approved plan before inspecting implementation outputs. `acceptance.json` records representative outcomes, not an assertion that each example is a complete fixture or that a single observed signal proves every condition. Full, partial and failure correspond to 1, 0.5 and 0; unknown remains unmeasured. Exclusions require the explicit evidence stated in the matrix. Where mixed evidence and unknown coexist, incomplete inspection must not be converted into a definitive aggregate result without evidence supporting it.

The cases are evaluated independently: vary the evidence for one subcheck without assuming deductions in another. In particular, shopper-content completeness, display agreement, rendered output, robots policy and unrelated rubric criteria are outside these revised checks. Complete absence and confirmed wrong relationships are distinct from missing, truncated, or unsupported inspection. A merchant SKU is partial identifier evidence, while no identifiers assigned requires affirmative evidence and no supplied external ID. Offers remain applicable for the current merchant categories.

Check local redistribution with both full and partial remaining measurements, unchanged other criteria, and no final score for an unmeasured applicable check or an entirely excluded criterion. Verify bounded inputs, truncation and repeated deterministic extraction, all supported markup formats, versioned cache isolation, historical v1 arithmetic and current API shape. Expected weights are S1 4/3/3/2/1 and S2 2/2/2/2/1, with criterion maxima 13 and 9. These examples and arithmetic are independently specified; no current implementation or generated report is a source of gold expectations.

Amendment before final v2 fixture expectations: the final approved proposal clarifies that Offer credit includes coherent structured price, currency and availability, while variant credit covers the parent family and distinguishing attributes; Offer relations are scored in Offer. This corrects the initial matrix's overly narrow Offer anchor and its misplaced variant Offer requirement. Missing structured fields are assessed as markup modeling, independently of shopper-facing content completeness.
