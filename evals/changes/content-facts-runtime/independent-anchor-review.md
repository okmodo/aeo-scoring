# Independent S6 and S3 anchor interpretation

Reviewed against the agreed-planning vault notes “AEO S6 Sitemap Discovery,” “AEO S3 Markup Agreement,” “AEO Scoring Contract,” and “AEO Assessment Evidence and Coverage.” This is an interpretation, not a change to rubric anchors or approval of an implementation.

## S6: no zero from one fallback

A successful robots inspection with no sitemap declarations and a confirmed 404 or 410 at `/sitemap.xml` proves those scoped observations. It does not prove that a usable discoverable sitemap or the product's inclusion in one is absent. The agreed S6 note calls for following declarations, indexes, and **common fallback locations**, says no particular filename is mandatory, and leaves budget-limited or incomplete inspection unmeasured. Another discoverable map may exist under another common name. Consequently, assigning zero to both checks solely from this pair of observations narrows the criterion and is not an implementation of the agreed anchor. Preserve `not_measured` and document the inspected locations and limits. An explicit, reviewed rubric change would be needed to score a deliberately narrower discovery scope.

A timeout, access denial, oversized or malformed response, uninspected index child, unprocessed declaration, or exhausted budget also prevents a negative inference. Confirmed absence applies only when the applicable search was actually complete; it does not require a claim that every possible hidden filesystem path was searched. A positive listing remains positive evidence even if other search is incomplete, while its evidence and coverage must be accurately described.

## S3: partial correspondence requires a meaningful comparison

The agreement anchor allows half for useful but incomplete or ambiguous correspondence and zero for material contradiction or missing required comparison fields. Same variant, market, currency, and purchase conditions matter, and the note specifically says unknown market or variant correspondence can require `not_measured`. A raw numeric price and associated structured price may support half where the inspected evidence establishes they concern a compatible product and leaves only an identifiable correspondence detail ambiguous; absent currency or selected-scope certainty alone cannot automatically establish compatibility, a contradiction, or a universal half. A confirmed missing required comparison field under complete relevant inspection may instead meet the explicit zero anchor. Failed inspection remains `not_measured`. Record the available fields, uncertainty, and why the selected anchor applies.

For an optional claim supplied in markup and an adequately inspected raw page without a corresponding claim, half can represent incomplete correspondence when the group is meaningfully comparable but there is no established contradiction. Do not label absence as a material conflict, and do not infer the claim is false. A confirmed contradictory claim may score zero; an uninspected or inadequately inspected relevant raw scope remains `not_measured`. The rubric also permits zero for missing required comparison fields, so do not generalize this optional-claim interpretation to required S3 fields. If no optional claims are supplied in markup, use the existing redistribution rule rather than deducting for their absence.

These interpretations are not evidence that any specific merchant inspection was complete, any report is ready, or any changed expected result is independently approved. Freeze context-specific cases and review them against the observations before changing expectations.

## S6: product-partition positive observation

The separately supplied fresh Kokatat publisher index lists same-origin children under `/xmlsitemap.php` for pages, products, categories, and news; the supplied `type=products&page=1` urlset contains the exact URL `https://kokatat.com/naiad-pfd-lvhnai`. The S6 note requires a parseable sitemap or index with usable relevant children and appropriate product inclusion. For these observations, recognizing the publisher's unambiguous product partition and examining its product child is consistent with that positive anchor: the unrelated page, category, and news partitions need not be inspected to establish this exact positive product inclusion.

Approve only this bounded approach in principle: validate the index and strict recognized same-origin endpoint and query shape, inspect every product child relevant to the usability judgment, and leave unresolved, failed, truncated, unrecognized, or budget-limited relevance as unknown. A positive exact inclusion is evidence of inclusion; it is not evidence that every partition was inspected or that other product maps cannot exist. Freeze sanitized index and child observations and exercise multiple product pages, failures, and unrecognized shapes before updating expected results. This is not approval of an implementation or evidence that a fresh audit reproduced these observations.

## Deterministic supplement review

The initial supplement has an independently reproduced false positive: with an empty model response, a single complete source observation `This product costs USD 25 to ship.` is automatically extracted as merchandise price USD 25 and C3.price receives full credit when a single product configuration is assumed. Shipping cost is not product price. The product-versus-shipping relationship must be established or remain unmeasured; add an adversarial regression before treating this implementation as reviewed. Literal role cues are not proof of subject, semantic relation, product applicability, or exhaustive absence. The separately authored 78.125 synthetic response is not live calibration.
