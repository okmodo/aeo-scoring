# AEO Verified Crawler Registry

The versioned registry and its official vendor evidence are canonical in [crawler policy](../../docs/crawler-policy.md) and [crawler registry](../../config/crawlers.v1.json). S5 assigns equal shares within the search and retrieval pools, and leaves unresolved outcomes unmeasured. Training-related restrictions and user-triggered fetchers do not deduct points. Vendor-specific robots handling and rendering capability are not interchangeable; a spoofed user-agent test does not prove actual crawler access.
