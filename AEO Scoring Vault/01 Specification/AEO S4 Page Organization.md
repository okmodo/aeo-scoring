# AEO S4 Page Organization

S4 allocates 3 points: descriptive title and identifiable main product heading (1); logically labeled sections and readable specifications (1); useful text alternatives for informative product images (1).

Full credit requires useful, specific labeling. Half credit applies to generic or incomplete labels. Confirmed missing or unusable labels receive zero. Multiple h1 elements alone do not fail the check. Tables, definition lists, and other clear text structures may all communicate specifications. Decorative images do not require descriptive product alternatives.
