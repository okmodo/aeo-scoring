# AEO Scoring Contract

The assessment totals 100 points: Structure 40 and Content 60. It measures accessibility, completeness, and consistency against our published rubric, not actual AI citations, traffic, ranking, or a probability of recommendation. Published guidance supports practices; numerical weights remain our editorial choices.

Use rubric v1 and a versioned category template. Unless a criterion states otherwise, each subcheck receives full credit for clear complete evidence, half for useful but materially incomplete or ambiguous evidence, and zero for confirmed absence, invalidity, contradiction, or unusability.

Use `not_measured` when evidence cannot be reliably obtained. Never convert failed inspection into zero. Use `not_applicable` only under an explicit category rule; the judge cannot invent exclusions. Redistribute excluded weight within its criterion and disclose the adjusted denominator. If an entire criterion has no applicable checks, withhold the overall score until a versioned rule defines treatment rather than silently reallocating across pillars.

Keep calculation precision and round only displayed criterion scores to one decimal. Derive overall totals from unrounded values. Withhold a final overall score while applicable scored checks remain unmeasured; show assessment status and earned points on completed checks without implying a final total.

Record audited URL, final URL, canonical, selected variant, market, currency, audit time, rubric version, category-template version, and extraction/judge version. Comparisons use compatible contexts and versions.

Each deduction needs quoted evidence or a documented absence check. AI applies defined anchors and locates evidence; it does not choose weights or fabricate product facts. Record evidence location and retrieval method. Give issues distinct scoring homes, and deduplicate recovery projections across shared fixes.
