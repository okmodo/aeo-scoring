# AEO S3 Markup Agreement

S3 allocates 9 points.

| Subcheck | Points |
|---|---:|
| Product identity and variant | 2 |
| Price and currency | 3 |
| Availability | 2 |
| Other supplied claims | 2 |

Compare the same variant, market, currency, and purchase conditions. Full credit means agreement, half means incomplete or ambiguous correspondence, and zero means material contradiction or missing required comparison fields.

Other supplied claims include descriptions, specifications, reviews, shipping, and returns. Average across applicable claim groups. Their absence does not itself incur a deduction: if no optional claims are supplied, redistribute those two points across the first three subchecks. No product markup means S3 scores zero; S2 and S3 together intentionally reserve 18 points for useful and trustworthy structured data.

Accept clearly explained sales, member prices, and legitimate ranges. Avoid comparing different variant prices as if contradictory. Unknown market or variant correspondence may require unmeasured status rather than an asserted mismatch.
