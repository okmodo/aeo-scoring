# AEO Robots Evidence Does Not Gate Audits

The audit fetches product pages and supporting content independently of robots.txt permissions. Robots.txt supplies optional evidence for crawler-policy scoring and sitemap discovery. A failed, malformed, slow or restrictive policy does not prevent the audit.

Public-destination validation, response limits and HTTP access barriers continue to govern actual retrieval. Unverified crawler-policy evidence remains unmeasured.
