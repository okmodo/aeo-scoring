# AEO C4 Decision Support

C4 allocates 10 points.

| Subcheck | Points |
|---|---:|
| Intended uses and audience | 3 |
| Compatibility, fit, operating requirements | 3 |
| Relevant limitations and exclusions | 2 |
| Size, variant, configuration selection guidance | 2 |

Use fixed category-specific questions. Full credit means applicable questions answered specifically; half means useful but incomplete answers; zero means absent or generic answers. Category templates must define question-level anchors where several questions contribute to one subcheck.

Answers can appear anywhere in readable product content. Do not mandate a FAQ section, five questions, competitor comparisons, or irrelevant limitations. Do not deduct twice for the same fit or selection question; define its scoring home in the template.
