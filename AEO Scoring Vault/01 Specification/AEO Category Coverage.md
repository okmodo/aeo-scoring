# AEO Category Coverage

The initial v1 templates cover running shoes, casual daypacks, and basic T-shirts. These editorial choices need Phase 2 calibration and do not establish product facts or legal compliance. Reliable classification against the documented scope and exclusions is required; otherwise C2 is unmeasured and no category-specific C4 checklist is invented.

C2 divides its essential and supporting pools among applicable fixed attributes. C4 uses separate fixed guidance questions with one subcheck home each. A value and actionable guidance are distinct evidence; a missing value is not deducted again as a duplicate guidance issue. A confirmed material contradiction is scored in C5 and does not alone reduce C2; independently missing or unusable attribute context is assessed in C2. Conditional exclusions require an observable rule and an established absent trigger; uncertainty is unmeasured.
