# AEO Assignable Fixes

One fix represents one coherent outcome with a primary owner, and supporting owners where needed. Owners are web team, content, catalog/data, and site operations. Team labels describe roles rather than contact information.

Each fix includes: action title; why it matters; affected subcheck IDs and current scores; source evidence; concrete recommended change; primary/supporting owners; potential recovery and its conditions; dependencies; confidence; and effort only when defensibly estimated.

Keep independent work separate. Adding missing source attributes and rendering existing attributes are different tasks unless a combined outcome is needed to earn credit. Related deductions can be grouped under one assignable action with each subcheck shown beneath it.

Never recommend invented product facts. Distinguish providing information from publishing information: catalog-only edits earn no page-content points until exposed on the page. Policy restrictions may be intentional; the action is review the policy before deciding to change it.
