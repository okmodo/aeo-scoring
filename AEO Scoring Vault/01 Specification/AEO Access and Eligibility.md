# AEO Access and Eligibility

Explain access separately from the numerical score: successful retrieval; login and challenge barriers; crawler-specific restrictions; indexing and snippet directives; canonical relationships; and initial-HTML versus rendered availability.

A restriction affecting one system does not mean all AI agents are blocked. Keep training, search retrieval, and user-triggered fetching distinct. Use confirmed, observed-test-only, and unconfirmed language appropriate to evidence.

A confirmed restriction receives a prominent warning adjacent to the score. No arbitrary 10/20/30 score caps apply. S5 deductions are not repeated elsewhere. A failed fetch is not definitive evidence about the real crawler's access. If the page cannot be assessed, show Unable to assess without a numerical score.

Indexability requirements must be attributed to the platform or feature they govern. A non-self canonical may be legitimate for variants. Resolve context before flagging it as incorrect. Access policy may be intentional: recommend review rather than automatic removal.
