# AEO Report Summary

Use a plain score without readiness bands, grades, or labels such as Invisible or AI-ready.

The top of a complete report shows product name, assessed URL, overall score /100, Structure /40, Content /60, assessed variant and market/currency, audit timestamp, and rubric version. Add a short evidence-based main takeaway describing the biggest obstacle.

Show material access restrictions immediately beside the score, even when the total is high. The score reflects readiness criteria, not measured citations or an endorsement of product truthfulness.

A report with missing applicable scored checks says Assessment incomplete and explains why; show completed findings and earned points without a final overall score. A retrieval failure says Unable to assess. Clearly label cached results with their actual audit time, not the time of opening the report.

The initial view should prioritize the summary, access warning where present, and first actionable fix. Deeper scoring detail remains expandable. No report design or application has been built.
