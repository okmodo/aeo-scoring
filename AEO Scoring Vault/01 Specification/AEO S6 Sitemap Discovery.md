# AEO S6 Sitemap Discovery

S6 allocates 2 points.

| Subcheck | Points | Full | Half |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Discoverable usable sitemap | 1 | Retrievable parseable sitemap or index with usable relevant children | Partly usable with broken relevant references |
| Appropriate product inclusion | 1 | Audited URL or legitimate canonical product URL appears | Listed entry has confirmed redirect or canonical conflict |

Confirmed absence earns zero. Budget-limited or incomplete inspection is unmeasured rather than missing. Follow Sitemap declarations in robots.txt, sitemap indexes, and common fallback locations; no particular filename is mandatory. Support bounded inspection of large and compressed sitemaps.

Report inspected coverage. Sitemap presence is a discovery practice, not a guarantee of indexing or a necessity for every small well-linked site. Freshness anomalies are diagnostic only: no points merely for lastmod, priority, or changefreq existing. A single visit cannot verify a plausible lastmod reflects the last significant change.
