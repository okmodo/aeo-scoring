# AEO C5 Consistency and Claim Support

C5 allocates 10 points: shopper-visible consistency (5) and material claim support (5).

For consistency, equally assess applicable groups: identity, physical specifications, suitability, and commercial facts. Full credit means no detected contradiction with sufficient comparable evidence; half means unresolved ambiguity; zero means a confirmed material contradiction. A fact stated once is not contradictory. Missing information belongs under completeness, and schema-versus-page conflicts belong under S3.

For claim support, average across identified material claims. Full credit requires relevant support, explanation, certification reference, or appropriate qualification; half means incomplete scope or relevance; zero means unsupported or contradicted by available evidence. If no claims requiring substantiation exist, exclude this subcheck under the applicability rule.

Ordinary specifications do not each need external citations. Quantified performance, certification, health, and safety claims need appropriate support. Promotional language is not automatically a fabricated factual claim. Evidence and severity definitions must be explicit before implementation.

The result means observed consistency and support, not independently verified accuracy. Every AI-detected issue requires a source quote and reason.
