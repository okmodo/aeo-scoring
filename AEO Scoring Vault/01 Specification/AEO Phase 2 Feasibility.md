# AEO Phase 2 Feasibility

A bounded offline snapshot prototype extracts a small set of visible and structured product observations with source context. Synthetic raw/rendered examples and a content-request contract demonstrate mechanics, not representative accuracy or a finished scorer. Real-page raw/rendered evidence, independent human calibration, and Cloudflare runtime and budget measurements remain gates before a production feasibility decision. Unobserved information is not proof of absence; page content and model output are untrusted.

Canonical implementation, measured limitations and replay instructions: [Phase 2 feasibility](../../docs/phase-2-feasibility.md). Cloudflare constraints: [Cloudflare feasibility](../../docs/cloudflare-feasibility.md).
