# AEO Recovery Calculation

For a subcheck:

**Potential recovery = points after the specified fix − current awarded points.**

The after score follows the same rubric, category template, applicability rules, and assessed context. The fix must explain the conditions needed to reach that score. Never calculate recovery from unmeasured evidence as though it were a known zero.

Sum the relevant subcheck differences for an action. Calculate a multi-action total by rescoring each affected subcheck once after all selected changes; do not simply add standalone action totals. If effects interact, evaluate the combined outcome rather than assume independence.

An action can show standalone recovery and, where overlapping, its additional recovery after preceding actions. Prefer merging overlapping actions or presenting them as alternatives. Do not display negative gains as recoverable points.

Use Potential recovery: +N when the specified change has a clear outcome. Use Up to +N only when the evidenced maximum and its conditions are explicit. Otherwise use Recovery not yet calculated. Avoid speculative ranges.

Dependencies share recovery: supplying attributes and publishing them may jointly earn points, but each step cannot claim the entire gain. Show one combined action or mark dependent steps and their shared total. A generated file alone earns no recovery until published.

A re-audit establishes actual change. All projections are conditional rubric points, never promises of traffic or citations. If rubric or applicability changes, the result is a new assessment rather than a directly comparable recovery claim.
