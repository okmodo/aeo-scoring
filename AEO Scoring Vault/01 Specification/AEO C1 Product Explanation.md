# AEO C1 Product Explanation

C1 allocates 10 points.

| Subcheck | Points |
|---|---:|
| Specific product identity, brand, model where applicable | 3 |
| Clear explanation of what the product is and does | 4 |
| Clear selected configuration and included items | 3 |

Specific, understandable, complete information earns full credit. Generic or materially incomplete descriptions earn half. Confirmed absence earns zero. Do not enforce a word count, opening-paragraph format, or answer-first template. Evaluate readable shopper-facing content, including rendered content; application-state blobs alone do not qualify.
