# AEO C3 Purchase Information

C3 allocates 10 points.

| Subcheck | Points |
|---|---:|
| Price, currency, material pricing conditions | 3 |
| Relevant configuration availability | 2 |
| Shipping coverage, cost or calculation method, delivery guidance | 3 |
| Return eligibility, window, material conditions | 2 |

Full credit requires clear applicable information. Half credit means useful information omits a material condition. Confirmed missing information earns zero.

A directly linked accessible policy may receive full credit when it clearly applies. Inspect its destination: a link alone receives no automatic credit. Clearly explained destination-dependent or checkout calculations can receive partial credit. Do not fabricate delivery dates or infer return conditions. Evaluate the selected market and configuration consistently.
