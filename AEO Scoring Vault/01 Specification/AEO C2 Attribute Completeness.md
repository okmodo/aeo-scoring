# AEO C2 Attribute Completeness

C2 allocates 20 points using a versioned category template established before assessment.

| Pool | Points |
|---|---:|
| Essential category attributes | 14 |
| Supporting category attributes | 6 |

Divide each pool equally across applicable attributes. An attribute earns full credit for an explicit value including units, scope, and variant association where required; half for useful but materially incomplete information; zero for missing or too-vague information.

Templates define applicability, essential versus supporting classification, accepted representations, and partial-credit anchors. Running-shoe examples include size, width, weight, drop, materials, intended surface, and care; these examples are not a finalized template.

If no approved template reliably matches the category, C2 is unmeasured. Do not let a model invent a checklist during the audit. Content scoring accepts rendered shopper-visible values; S1 separately evaluates their delivery. Never invent product values to fill gaps.
