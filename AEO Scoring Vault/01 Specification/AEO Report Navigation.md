# AEO Report Navigation

Present report sections in this order:

1. Assessment summary: score, assessed context, completion, warnings.
2. Main takeaway: the most consequential evidenced obstacle.
3. Priority fixes: initially three assignable actions with View all.
4. Structure and content breakdown: criterion scores and short observations.
5. Crawler access: allowed, restricted, and unconfirmed outcomes by system.
6. Evidence and coverage: inspected resources and limits.
7. Downloads and next actions.

Group fixes as assignable actions, not individual failed checks. Expand each criterion into its subchecks, evidence, and rules. Keep successful checks available. Filters include Needs attention, Passed, Structure, Content, and Owner. Optional Quick wins can reorder by defensible effort estimates; default ordering follows the priority rules.

Report copy must distinguish information absent from a page from information present but difficult to retrieve. Do not repeat all scoring machinery in the first screen.
